# TapQ Product Vision

> **AI should come to you without pulling you back to a screen.**

TapQ turns devices people already wear — starting with AirPods — into a private interaction layer for AI.

Today, TapQ lets developers supervise AI coding agents hands-free: hear a prompt, answer by voice, or respond with a head gesture. The longer-term vision extends the same interaction model beyond the terminal into meetings, lectures, and travel.

## Today: hands-free AI agent control

TapQ currently works with coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and OpenCode on macOS. When an agent needs attention, TapQ can speak the request through AirPods and accept a response through voice or motion.

Examples:

- nod to approve,
- shake to deny,
- move through choices with head motion or AirPods controls,
- ask what an agent changed,
- give a follow-up instruction without reopening the terminal.

The goal is simple: **keep the agent moving while the user stays focused.**

## Where TapQ is going

The same interaction model can make AI useful in moments where opening a phone or speaking a wake phrase feels distracting or unnatural.

| Experience | Status | What TapQ is exploring |
|---|---|---|
| **Developer Agent Mode** | Available / evolving | Hands-free supervision of AI coding agents |
| **Business Mode** | Planned | Private, real-time contextual assistance during meetings |
| **Lecture Mode** | Planned | In-class clarification, structured notes, and review |
| **Travel Mode** | Vision | Context-aware translation and assistance while moving |

## The interaction model

TapQ is built around four ideas:

1. **Private output** — useful information arrives in your ear.
2. **Low-friction input** — respond with a nod, shake, tap, or short voice reply.
3. **Context awareness** — assistance should reflect what is happening now, not just generic knowledge.
4. **No extra wearable** — use hardware people already own whenever possible.

TapQ is not trying to put another chatbot on a smaller screen. The goal is to make AI feel like a quiet layer around the work, conversation, or environment already in front of you.
