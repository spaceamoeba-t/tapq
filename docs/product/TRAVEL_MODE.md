# Travel Mode

**Status: Vision**

> **Translation is useful. Understanding the situation is better.**

Travel Mode is TapQ's longer-term vision for hands-free, context-aware assistance across languages.

## Beyond literal translation

Real-time translation is increasingly available across phones, earbuds, and dedicated devices. TapQ does not need to become another translation engine.

The product opportunity is to add **context and action** on top of translation.

A traveler could ask:

- “What did they mean by that?”
- “Was that price per person or total?”
- “What is this dish?”
- “Tell them I have a reservation under Wang.”
- “Summarize the instructions they just gave me.”

## A hands-free interaction

The experience should minimize the need to hold a phone between two people.

TapQ could use earbuds for:

- private translated audio,
- short contextual explanations,
- confirmation through head gestures,
- voice-generated responses in the local language.

The goal is a conversation that feels closer to normal human interaction, using hardware the traveler already carries.

## Why it comes later

Translation itself is already a competitive, fast-moving capability. TapQ's differentiation is stronger where it combines:

**wearable input + contextual understanding + private assistance + AI action.**

Travel Mode therefore follows validation of the core interaction model in developer, business, and learning workflows.
