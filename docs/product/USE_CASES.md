# TapQ Use Cases

TapQ is designed for moments when AI can help, but looking at a screen breaks attention.

## 1. Supervise AI agents while away from the terminal

**Status: Available / evolving**

An AI coding agent stops for approval while you are reading, walking around the office, or working on another task.

Instead of reopening the terminal:

- TapQ speaks the request in your AirPods,
- you nod, shake, or answer by voice,
- the response is sent back to the agent,
- the agent keeps working.

**Value:** less context switching while keeping the human in control.

---

## 2. Understand a meeting without reaching for your phone

**Status: Planned — Business Mode**

Someone mentions an unfamiliar acronym, metric, company, or domain-specific concept in a meeting. You do not want to interrupt the speaker or visibly search on your phone.

TapQ could use the recent meeting context to privately offer help:

> “Want a quick explanation of CAC payback?”

Nod yes, and TapQ gives a short contextual explanation in your ear. Shake no, or do nothing, and the meeting continues without interruption.

Afterward, the same session context can support a structured recap of key topics, decisions, action items, and terminology.

## 3. Stay with the professor when a concept is unclear

**Status: Planned — Lecture Mode**

A professor introduces a concept you do not fully understand, but searching for it risks missing the next five minutes of the lecture.

TapQ could:

- preserve the lecture context,
- provide a short explanation when requested,
- mark difficult concepts for later review,
- organize the session into notes and key ideas after class.

The goal is not to replace listening. It is to help students **stay in the lecture instead of leaving it to search.**

[Learn more about Lecture Mode →](LECTURE_MODE.md)

---

## 4. Understand more than the literal translation

**Status: Vision — Travel Mode**

While traveling, literal translation is often only the first step. Users also need context:

- “Was that price per person or total?”
- “What did they mean by that phrase?”
- “Tell them I have a reservation under Wang.”
- “Summarize the directions they just gave me.”

TapQ's long-term travel experience is about combining translation with conversational context and AI assistance — through earbuds the user already has.
