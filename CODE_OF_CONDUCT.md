# Code of Conduct

TapQ Open is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop an interaction.
- Help create an environment where newcomers can participate safely.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexualized attention or content directed at another participant.
- Publishing another person’s private information without permission.
- Personal attacks or retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
threads, project events, and other spaces where someone represents the TapQ Open
community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method
published in the repository’s Security tab or on a maintainer’s profile. Do not
open a public issue when doing so would expose a target or sensitive details.

Maintainers will review reports in confidence, avoid unnecessary disclosure,
and choose a response proportionate to the circumstances. Responses may include
a private warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or permanent
exclusion from project spaces. Maintainers who have a conflict of interest must
recuse themselves from handling the report.
